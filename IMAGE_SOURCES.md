## Verification details

Images are served from the local project, not dependent on third-party URLs at runtime. The Pexels CDN resized the original photographs to the delivered resolution. All final assets are below 470 KB.

Visual checking caught two misleading source titles: `cream-knit.jpg` depicts a bow-tie knit vest, and `ceramic-cup.jpg` depicts a pour-over coffee dripper. Product naming follows the actual pictures. Prominently branded candidate shoes, bags, and accessories were excluded. The hero photo may also appear on the same trench-coat product; no image is assigned to unrelated products.

The app provides a graceful local fallback if a file cannot load. A fallback indicates an unavailable image and is not presented as product photography.
